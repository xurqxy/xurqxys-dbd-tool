# Changelog

## 1.7.0
- **Sound effects** for spins, landing slots, locks, Mystery reveals and achievements. Switch them off or set the
  volume in Settings → Appearance. All sounds are made by the app itself; nothing is copied from the game.
- **CPU saver** switch in Settings: turns off background effects and decorative animations.
- **New installer**: you choose where to install (default: your AppData folder) and see the license first.
- **Clean uninstall**: removes everything, including the leftover installer copy in `%LOCALAPPDATA%`, and asks whether
  to also delete your saved matches and settings. Updating never deletes your data.
- **New app icon.**
- License, third-party notices and privacy policy included with the app.

## 1.6.0
- **Mobile app**: Android app (.apk) and a web app for iPhone/Android that you add to your home screen.
- Your own win rate shown on each rolled perk and character; perk stats can be filtered by character.
- **Popular builds** on the Builds page.
- Tamper protection: obfuscated builds, the desktop app refuses to start if its files were edited.

## 1.5.1
- Much lower CPU use (none while in the background), tidier title bar, one-click installer.

## 1.5.0
- Share codes and share images, 24 achievements, one-click updates.

## 1.4.x
- Buy me a coffee and GitHub links, update check, single-file exe.
- A chosen killer/survivor stays still while the rest spins.

## 1.3
- Pick your killer or survivor, real killer add-ons from the DBD Wiki, roster updated to October 2026.

## 1.2

## 1.0 – 1.1
