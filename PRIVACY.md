# Privacy

**Short version: Xurqxys DBD Tool doesn't collect anything about you.**

## What stays on your device
Everything you enter or the app records — matches, builds, settings, achievements and downloaded icons — is saved
only on your own device:

- **Windows:** in `%APPDATA%\Xurqxys DBD Tool`
- **Android app / web app:** in the app's private storage on the phone

None of it is uploaded anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## When the app goes online
Only for these things, and only to these places:

| What | Where | What's sent |
|---|---|---|
| Game data (add-ons, new perks) and icons | the community Dead by Daylight Wiki (`deadbydaylight.wiki.gg`, fallback `deadbydaylight.fandom.com`) | normal web requests for public wiki pages |
| Checking for a new version | GitHub (`api.github.com`) | a normal web request for the latest release |
| Links you click (Buy me a coffee, GitHub) | open in your browser | nothing from the app |

Those sites see an ordinary request (like visiting them in a browser), including your IP address, under their own
privacy policies. The app sends them nothing else.

**Streamer mode** runs a small server on your own PC (`127.0.0.1:7373`) so OBS can show the overlay. It's only reachable
from your own computer.

## Removing your data
- **Windows:** uninstall the app and answer **Yes** when it asks whether to delete your saved data. Or use
  Settings → Backup to export it first.
- **Android:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.
- **Web app:** remove it from the home screen and clear the site's data in your browser settings.
